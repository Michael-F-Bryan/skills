# Notes and updates (Michael)

**Scope:** engineering notes, personal/log notes (Obsidian, work logs), and company-facing updates. These share one property: the reader needs preserved or transferred state, not performance. If the artefact grows into a decision brief or report, switch to [operator-artifacts.md](operator-artifacts.md).

## Engineering notes

**Job:** preserve facts, assumptions, trade-offs, uncertainty, and decision state for teammates or maintainers.

- Lead with what changed or was discovered.
- Separate fact from assumption when the distinction matters.
- Preserve verification state and unresolved uncertainty — "tested on bench, not in water" is load-bearing information.
- Use headings for a substantial artefact, not because every thought needs a label.

Avoid: over-polish, vague status, ungrounded conclusions.

## Personal/log notes

**Job:** preserve useful context for future Michael without public-performance gloss.

- Write for retrieval: who, what, why it mattered, and which assumption changed.
- Keep it plain and add useful wikilinks.
- Preserve existing vault conventions instead of inventing a fresh format.

Avoid: productivity-blog reflection, stale labels, motivational gloss, "lessons learned" framing that pushes the note toward polished performance.

## Company-facing updates

**Job:** transfer material state, risk, or next steps in seconds.

- Include only what materially changes the reader's understanding of state, risk, or next steps.
- Terse and high-signal; optimise for "useful in ten seconds".
- Labels and bullets are useful when they improve scanning, not as a fixed template.
- Whole-company audience: plain English, explain acronyms the audience won't share.

Avoid: release-note boilerplate, decorative `Action:` labels, emoji unless something is genuinely on fire, cleverness.

## Quick check

- Could the intended reader (a teammate, future Michael, or the whole company) act on this without asking a follow-up?
- Did I preserve the assumption or uncertainty that will matter later?
- Is the ceremony level matched to the destination — plain for notes, terse for updates?
